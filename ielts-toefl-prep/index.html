About the App

IELTS & TOEFL Vocabulary Builder is an offline, self-paced academic vocabulary and exam preparation app designed for IELTS and 2026 TOEFL iBT test takers.

Key features include:

  • 2,000+ Academic Words: High-frequency academic word list with CEFR difficulty levels, contextual example sentences, definitions, and collocations.
  • Spaced Repetition System (SRS): Intelligent review scheduling that reinforces words right before you forget them.
  • IELTS Practice Tasks: Listening Sentence Dictation, Summary Completion, Sentence Completion, and True / False / Not Given reading comprehension.
  • New 2026 TOEFL iBT Tasks: Complete the Words, Build a Sentence, and Listen & Repeat.
  • Mini Mock Tests: 20-minute timed simulation tests calculating estimated IELTS band scores (1.0–9.0) and TOEFL scaled scores (1.0–6.0) with comprehensive section breakdowns.
  • Speech Practice: Offline pronunciation and repetition drills scored directly on your device.

Restoring Purchases

If you previously purchased a subscription or lifetime unlock and want to restore it on a new or existing device:

  1. Make sure you are signed into your device with the same Apple ID or Google Play account used to make the purchase.
  2. Open IELTS & TOEFL Prep.
  3. Tap the Settings icon in the top right corner of the Today screen.
  4. Tap Restore Purchases.
  5. Alternatively, tap Restore in the top right corner of any premium upgrade screen.

Your purchases will be verified and your premium access restored immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use the app offline?
A: Yes! All vocabulary lists, practice tasks, audio sentences, and mock test scoring run 100% offline without requiring an internet connection.

Q: How do I change my target exam?
A: Go to Settings → Target Exam, then select IELTS Academic, IELTS General Training, or TOEFL iBT. Your daily plan and mock tests will adapt automatically.

Q: How are scores calculated?
A: Mock test scores are estimated practice bands based on official scoring scales (IELTS 1.0–9.0 half-band scale and TOEFL 1.0–6.0 half-point scale). They provide feedback on your progress and are not official test scores.
